Tip ↴

  • Cover the English column with your hand, read only the French, and say the meaning out loud before you check.
  • Job titles like avocat(e), enseignant(e), and ingénieur(e) change form for a woman doing that job — learn both forms, not just the one that comes to mind first.
  • recycler shows up in the grammar reference too — it's an -er verb, so it conjugates exactly like travailler or gagner once you know the pattern.
