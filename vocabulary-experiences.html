Tip ↴

  • À, au, en, aux all mean "to/in" for places, but which one you use depends on the country's gender — worth checking once you hit Grammar Notes.
  • For food and hobbies, practise saying what YOU actually eat and do at the weekend — personal, true sentences stick far better than random examples.
